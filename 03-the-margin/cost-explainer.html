Why a season pass, and what the soft cap is

Why a season pass

Coaches prep in bursts: a bye week is quiet, a playoff week is heavy. A monthly meter would charge unevenly and make coaches ration the chat. A pass pools the whole season, so they use it when the games are.

It is outcome-based: they pay for being prepared for every game, not for tokens or chat turns.

What the soft cap is

A threshold that changes behaviour, not a wall. A coach who keeps iterating is often telling us the first draft was not good enough, so we ask what was missed instead of stopping them.
