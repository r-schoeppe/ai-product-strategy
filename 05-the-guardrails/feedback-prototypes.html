TapeMine Feedback Concepts

Three ways for a coach to say "this suggestion doesn't fit", on the same gameplan sheet. Try each one, then read the log on the right: that is what we would learn from. Mock data.

A: an explicit "Doesn't fit" on every suggestion

One tap, an optional reason, and the coach can still write their own version. The most direct form of feedback.

WHAT THE OWNER SEES
nothing yet

B: no button, the coach just edits and we notice

Edit the share or the suggestion directly. Small edits are saved quietly. A big edit gets one non-blocking "why?" with quick answers, which can be ignored.

WHAT THE OWNER SEES
nothing yet

C: choose between options, the choice is the feedback

Each section offers the suggestion and an alternative from the coach's playbook. Picking one teaches us the preference without any form. After the game, one tap records what worked.

My take: A and B together cover what you described. B is the quiet background trigger, A is the visible "doesn't fit" voice that makes coaches feel heard. C is the most powerful for learning, but it needs a populated playbook, so it is a later step.
Open question for you: how visible should the "we noticed your edit" in B be? Fully silent is simplest, but a small "noted" marker is what makes the coach trust that feedback exists.
